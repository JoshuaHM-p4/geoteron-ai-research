GEOTERON (working name)

INFRASTRUCTURE DECISION INTELLIGENCE

An infrastructure intelligence system that lets developers see the consequences of a project before, during, and after they build it.

&nbsp;

GEOTERON simulates development scenarios and shows their measurable trade-offs across five dimensions, with every result traceable to its assumptions, data, and model

Can a real engineer look at GEOTERON's result and say, "Yes, I understand where this number came from, and I trust it enough to use it as one input to a decision"?&nbsp;

Behavioral \- How people behave and move because of the development.

- Traffic.  
- Travel patterns.  
- Route changes.  
- Pedestrian behavior.  
- Accessibility.  
- Worker commuting.  
- Consumer movement.  
- Potential modal shifts.

Societal \- How communities are affected.

- Access to services.  
- Population distribution.  
- Displacement.  
- Accessibility inequality.  
- Employment distribution.  
- Public service pressure.  
- Quality-of-life indicators.

Environmental \- The physical environmental consequences.

- Water consumption.  
- Air quality.  
- Carbon emissions.  
- Energy demand.  
- Noise.  
- Waste.  
- Heat.  
- Flood risk.  
- Resource consumption.

Ecological \- The effects on ecosystems and natural systems

- Land cover.  
- Habitat.  
- Biodiversity.  
- Vegetation.  
- Water ecosystems.  
- Ecological fragmentation.  
- Watershed effects.  
- Ecosystem services.

Economic \- The financial and economic consequences.

- Development cost.  
- Infrastructure cost.  
- Operating cost.  
- Land value.  
- Economic activity.  
- Employment.  
- Tax/revenue potential.  
- Business activity.  
- ROI.  
- Opportunity cost.

&nbsp;

Environmental ≠ Ecological.

One concerns the physical environment surrounding humans and infrastructure.

The other concerns natural ecosystems.

Simulations: Before, During, After

BEFORE: Development Intelligence | Should we build this?

* Feasibility.  
* Site selection.  
* Impact prediction.  
* Scenario analysis.  
* Optimization.

&nbsp;

DURING: Construction Intelligence | Are we building it correctly?

* Progress.  
* Resource utilization.  
* Environmental monitoring.  
* Construction risk.  
* Schedule impact.  
* Deviation from expected impact.

&nbsp;

AFTER: Operational Intelligence | Did the development actually behave as expected?

* Actual vs predicted.  
* Population.  
* Traffic.  
* Economic activity.  
* Environmental conditions.  
* Ecological indicators.  
* Infrastructure utilization.

&nbsp;

Prediction → Construction → Reality → Learning → Better Prediction
